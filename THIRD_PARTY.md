# Third-party components and data

- Leaflet 1.9.4: BSD-2-Clause. Unmodified browser files and license: `src/vendor/leaflet/`.
- Background map: © OpenStreetMap contributors. Attribution remains visible. Standard OSM tiles require the internet and are requested only for the interactive viewport. No tile prefetch or offline tile cache is implemented.
- Fonts: DM Sans and Noto Sans KR, served by Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License.
- GPX: personal recordings supplied by the repository owner. The owner requested publication of both current and historical records.
- Itinerary: owner-supplied 2026-11-01 itinerary and date-specific KML place coordinates. Only derived schedule/place metadata is included; PDF and contact details are not bundled.
